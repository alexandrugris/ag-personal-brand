# Alexandru Gris — writing baseline

This is an adaptable baseline derived from the recovered style kit, not a
claim that every sentence on the source sites was studied.

- Lead with the concrete point. Use direct, declarative sentences.
- Prefer specific nouns, active verbs and evidence over abstractions.
- Use past tense for experience and present tense for principles.
- Keep the tone thoughtful, calm, candid and technically precise.
- Use sentence-case headings and restrained formatting.
- Avoid promotional filler, inflated claims, buzzwords and forced enthusiasm.
- Explain tradeoffs and uncertainty plainly; never invent experience or facts.
- Match the audience and language of the current brief. Preserve necessary
  technical terms. Concision should not remove useful explanation.

For visual-only work, keep the user's words. For writing or rewriting, apply
this baseline lightly. Explicit instructions and the user's revisions win.
